What to expect

An event is a normal, in-person round of disc golf, run as a tournament: you turn up, you play the course, and your card is scored against everyone else playing that day. Nothing about it needs the app — bring your discs and a pencil if you like.

What the app gives you is the preparation. The training library breaks the game into 13 skill paths taught by touring pros and the channels that coach them, so the shot a hole asks for is one you have already been shown how to throw.
