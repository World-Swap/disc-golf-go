Business Model
🎒 Vault Shop
XP boosts, Gold multipliers, Drop-Rate upgrades, cosmetic items. High-margin digital goods. No logistics, no inventory.
⚡ Premium Tier · $5/mo
Exclusive rotating quest tiers, premium battle modes, early access to seasonal content. Low price, high retention.
🆓 Zero Barrier Entry
F2P core game removes every acquisition friction. No $90/yr wall. Players download, check in, and convert at their own pace.

Battles, crew wars, leaderboards, and shareable round cards create compounding social loops. More players → more competition → more spend.
